# 📺 Finding YouTube Videos with Japanese Transcripts

Your Firebase Function is working perfectly! The issue is simply finding videos that have Japanese transcripts enabled.

## ✅ How to Find Videos with Japanese Subtitles

### Method 1: Check for CC Icon
1. Search YouTube for Japanese content
2. Look for the **CC** (Closed Captions) icon on video thumbnails
3. Click on the video, then click the **CC** button to verify Japanese subtitles are available

### Method 2: Check in Video Player
1. Open any YouTube video
2. Click the **Settings** gear icon ⚙️
3. Click **Subtitles/CC**
4. If you see **Japanese** or **日本語** in the list, it has transcripts!

### Method 3: Use YouTube Search Filters
In YouTube search, add these parameters to your URL:
```
&sp=EgIoAQ%253D%253D
```
This filters for videos with closed captions.

## ⚠️ Common Issues

### "Transcripts are disabled for this video"
This means:
- ✅ Your Firebase Function is working
- ❌ That specific video doesn't have transcripts
- 💡 Try a different video

### How to Verify a Video Has Transcripts

**Before using the app, test manually:**
1. Open the YouTube video
2. Click the three dots (**...**) below the video
3. Click **Show transcript**
4. If you see text appear, it has transcripts! ✅
5. If it says "No transcript available", try a different video ❌
